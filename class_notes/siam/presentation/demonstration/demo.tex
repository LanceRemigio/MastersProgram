\begin{definition}[Convergence of a Sequence in \( \R \)]
   We say that \( ({x}_{n}) \) converges to some \( x \in \R  \) if for all \( \epsilon > 0  \) , there exists an \(N \in \N  \) such that for all \( n > N  \) 
   \[  | {x}_{n} - x  |  < \epsilon. \]
\end{definition}
